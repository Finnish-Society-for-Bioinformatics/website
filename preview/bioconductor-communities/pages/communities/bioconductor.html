The Finnish Bioconductor community

What is Bioconductor?

Bioconductor is an open-source, open-development project that provides thousands of R packages for the analysis and comprehension of biological data, such as genomics, transcriptomics, single-cell, proteomics, metabolomics, and microbiome data. Behind the software is a welcoming, international community of users and developers who share code, teach courses, and meet at conferences.

Visit Bioconductor

The Finnish Bioconductor community

The Finnish Society for Bioinformatics brings together people in Finland who use or develop Bioconductor. Everyone is welcome — whether you have just installed your first package or maintain several of them.

Come and meet the community at the BioBeers and our other events. We are also planning Bioconductor-related events, such as hackathons.

Would you like to help organise them? Join the organising committee by contacting the society at fsfbioinf@gmail.com or on Slack.
